01 The gap

Your teams are already using AI. Can you see it?

Copilots, chat tabs, IDE assistants, MCP servers and autonomous agents arrive faster than security can review them. Existing tools see separate pieces. They rarely show who used what, what was sent, which rule applied, and what actually happened.

◇

Unseen usage

You cannot manage what you cannot name: the AI app, the model, the user, the device and the access it had.

⚠

Agents without clear owners

An agent can be given credentials and broad access without a clear owner, approval trail or fast way to stop it.

∅

No memory, no proof

When an incident happens or an auditor asks, teams should not have to rebuild the story from screenshots and logs.

03 The platform

See it. Decide. Act. Prove it.

Statefold links the endpoint with one central console. It finds AI use across supported apps and traffic paths, applies rules where a control is installed, and clearly says whether an event was stopped, only seen, or could not be checked.

StatefoldAgent.exe

Endpoint control

Finds AI activity, identifies the user or process, and applies rules on supported traffic before data is sent. Saved rules keep working if the console is briefly unavailable.

StatefoldConsole.exe

Central console

One view of AI use, identity, rules, evidence, risk and model reviews. Each customer or business unit keeps its data separate.
