01 The gap

Shadow AI is already inside your perimeter.

Copilots, chat tabs, IDE assistants, MCP servers and autonomous agents arrive faster than security can review them. The old stack was built for files and networks — not for prompts, tokens, and agents that act on their own.

◇

Unseen usage

You can't name the AI tools running on your endpoints, who runs them, or with what privilege. Discovery stops at the network edge — and the AI lives past it.

⚠

Ungoverned agents

Anyone can spin up an agent and hand it credentials. Nothing stops a malformed prompt, an over-privileged tool, or a quiet exfiltration to a model API.

∅

No memory, no model

Prompts, answers, code, MCP calls, tokens and spend evaporate. When an incident lands there's nothing to ask — and no intelligence that's actually yours.

A light sensor on the endpoint. A console in command.

Two executables. The agent enforces locally and keeps protecting even when disconnected. The console gives operators fleet-wide discovery, policy, governance, the Hive Mind and the Swarm — isolated per tenant for MSSPs and global enterprises alike.

Endpoint sensor

Observes every AI surface — proxy, browser, IDE, MCP, Bedrock, clipboard, DNS — classifies content with a pure-Python engine, and enforces on the box, before send.

Management console

Fleet telemetry, central policy, AI-agent governance, discovery, coverage, the Hive Mind and the Swarm — one API, an embedded store, multi-tenant by design.

05 Built to be trusted

Powerful by design. Quiet by default.

No LLM dependency

Classification, policy and governance are rule-based and deterministic. The SLM you grow sits on top of your own data — never an external API in the trust path.

Enforced before-send

The endpoint redacts and blocks in-process, before a request is SigV4-signed. No network tool can rewrite a signed request — Statefold never lets it form.

Offline-safe

Fleet sync is additive. Disconnect the endpoint and protection continues — never a dependency for safety.

Agent & privilege kill

An agent runs only if it and the privilege it seeks are both approved. Otherwise it's halted and killed.

Multi-tenant isolation

One console, many isolated client enterprises — separate fleets, policy, Hive Mind and SLM. Built for MSSPs and global org boundaries.

Your data stays yours

The Hive Mind and your SLM are local. Prompts, tokens, spend and gradients never leave the perimeter.

06 How it works

Five moves to AI under control.

  1. 01

    Enroll

    Drop the light agent on endpoints; it self-registers to the console with a scoped, tenant-bound token.

  2. 02

    Observe

    Every AI surface is discovered and classified, attributed to a user, endpoint and privilege.

  3. 03

    Govern

    Approve agents and privileges separately; policy and grants enforce at the endpoint, before send.

  4. 04

    Ask

    The Hive Mind remembers it all. Ask in plain language; get a deterministic answer.

  5. 05

    Grow

    The Swarm distills your own SLM from it — and keeps teaching itself as the fleet works.
